# Contributing to MdPreview

Thank you for your interest in contributing to **MdPreview**!

## How You Can Contribute

1. **Bug Reports & Feature Suggestions**:
   - Please open an issue via our [Issue Templates](https://github.com/tangtang-it/mdpreview/issues).
   - Provide concrete steps to reproduce or clear use-case details.

2. **Feedback & Ideas**:
   - Join the discussion on existing feature requests or suggest new card templates, export formats, and workflow integrations.

3. **Spread the Word**:
   - If you enjoy using MdPreview, consider starring this repository or sharing it with your network!
