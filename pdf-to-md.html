Frequently Asked Questions

Is my data uploaded to any remote server?

No. MDPreview runs 100% locally in your browser memory. We do not store, log, or upload your PDF files to any cloud server.

Can it convert scanned image-only PDFs?

This tool is designed for text-based PDFs (generated from Word, LaTeX, Google Docs, Notion, or export tools). Scanned image-only PDFs require OCR processing.

Is there a file size limit?

Since processing occurs directly in your computer's RAM, files up to 50MB and hundreds of pages convert with lightning speed without server limits.

Is this tool free to use?

Yes, MDPreview PDF to MD is completely free and open-source with no registration or subscription required.
