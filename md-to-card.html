Frequently Asked Questions

Where are the exported cards hosted?

Nowhere! Card images are generated 100% locally in your browser memory and downloaded directly to your device. We do not store or track your content.

Can I use these cards for commercial projects?

Yes. Any image or card you generate with MDPreview is 100% yours to publish, share, and monetize without attribution or restrictions.

Does it support code syntax highlighting?

Yes, code blocks with language tags (e.g. `javascript, `python, ` ust) are highlighted using highlight.js with beautiful styling.

What aspect ratio should I use for Xiaohongshu?

We recommend using the "Portrait 3:4" aspect ratio for Xiaohongshu and Instagram stories for the best vertical viewing experience.
